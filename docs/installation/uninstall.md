# Uninstall

## Remove only your data

To keep EarsUp but clear what it stored, open the settings panel and go to **About**:

- **Remove key and history** deletes your Gemini key and every history entry from this browser.
- **Reset** puts every setting back to its default. It keeps your key and history.

## Remove EarsUp

1. Open `chrome://extensions`.
2. Click **Remove** on the EarsUp card and confirm.
3. Delete the folder you loaded it from.

Removing the extension also removes everything it stored: settings, Gemini key, and history. Nothing is kept on a server, because EarsUp has no server.
