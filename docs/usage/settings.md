# Settings panel

Click the EarsUp icon in the toolbar. The settings open in Chrome's side panel, on the right of the page, so you can change things while you listen.

</div>

In the side panel the sections are tabs along the top. When the panel is wide, or when you open it in a full tab, they move to a sidebar on the left.

![Settings in a full tab](../media/settings-general-light.png){ .shot }

## Sections

| Section | What is in it |
|---------|---------------|
| **General** | EarsUp on/off, heading levels, button position and visibility, section highlight, [follow along](follow-along.md), floating player, theme, [keys](keys.md) |
| **Voices** | [Voice engine](voices.md), speed, pitch, volume, Chrome voices, Gemini connection |
| **Sites** | [Where EarsUp runs](sites.md) |
| **History** | [Everything you listened to](history.md) |
| **About** | How it works, privacy, version, reset |

Every button and control has a tooltip. Point at it, or move to it with ++tab++, to see what it does.

## Theme

**General → Appearance → Theme**: System, Light, or Dark. System follows your operating system's setting.

## Syncing

Your settings sync to other computers where you are signed in to Chrome with sync on. Your Gemini key and history do not sync. They stay in this browser.
