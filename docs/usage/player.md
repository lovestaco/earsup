# Floating player

While EarsUp reads, a small player sits at the bottom of the page. It shows the heading being read, how far along it is, and the controls.

![The floating player on a dark page](../media/reading-dark.png){ .shot }

## Controls

| Control | What it does |
|---------|--------------|
| <span class="twemoji">:material-skip-previous:</span> | Go back one part |
| <span class="twemoji">:material-pause:</span> / <span class="twemoji">:material-play:</span> | Pause or resume |
| <span class="twemoji">:material-skip-next:</span> | Skip to the next part |
| **1×** <span class="twemoji">:material-chevron-down:</span> | Choose the reading speed |
| <span class="twemoji">:material-close:</span> | Stop reading and close the player |

A "part" is about one or two sentences. Long sections are read in parts so speech does not cut out.

Every control has a tooltip. Point at it to see what it does.

## Speed

Click the speed button to open the list: 0.5×, 0.75×, 0.9×, 1×, 1.1×, 1.25×, 1.5×, 1.75×, 2×, 2.5×. You can use the arrow keys and **Enter** in the list, and **Esc** closes it.

The speed you choose here is saved. It is the same setting as **Voices → Playback → Speed**.

![Speed menu open on the player](../media/speed-menu.png){ .shot }

## Move it

Drag the player anywhere on the page. EarsUp remembers the spot for next time. Double-click the player to put it back at the bottom centre.

## Turn it off

If you only want the play buttons, turn off **General → Reading → Floating player**. You can still pause and stop with the [keys](keys.md).
